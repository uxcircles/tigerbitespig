The name

In Taiwan, a pork-belly bao is called hǔ yǎo zhū, “tiger bites pig”.

Fold a soft bun around slow-braised pork and it looks like a mouth mid-bite. Our bao follow that Taipei street-food tradition. Our rice bowls lean west, towards the chilli and numbing pepper of Sichuan.

  1. 01

    Made here, the long way

    Service is quick, but the cooking isn’t. We ferment our own kimchi, pickle in-house and braise meat overnight in our own master stock.

  2. 02

    Rolled fresh, every day

    Our signature bao are hand-rolled and steamed in our own kitchen, fresh every day.

  3. 03

    Sourced close to home

    We buy meat and fresh produce from local suppliers and British producers who share our values. Supporting the local supply chain matters to us.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Anything else? Email hello@tigerbitespig.co.uk

Do I need to book?

We recommend it, especially on weekends, but we keep some tables for walk-ins. We take card details as a £10 per person hold when you book. Nothing is charged unless you don’t show up or break our booking terms. We hold tables for 15 minutes.

Can you cater for vegans?

Yes. Most bao and rice bowls can be made with “This Isn’t Chicken” or tofu. Look for VG and VGO on the menu, or download our vegan menu.

Do you have gluten-free options?

Our bao aren’t gluten-free, but several rice bowls, salads and sides can be. Look for GF and GFO, and tell your server about any allergies.

Can I order takeaway or delivery?

Yes. Click & collect on Good Eats , delivery on Deliveroo , or order to take away at our kiosk.

Large groups or private hire?

Yes, we’d love to have you. Email hello@tigerbitespig.co.uk with your date, time and number of people.

Are you dog friendly?

Small, well-behaved, non-barking dogs are very welcome.

How can I pay?

Card only. We don’t take cash or cheques.

Independent Birmingham discount?

Yes. Members get 10% off bills of £10 or more, Monday to Thursday.
