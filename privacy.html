3. Services we work with

Some services are run by other companies. Where they act on our behalf (as “processors”), they may only use your data to provide that service to us:

  • DesignMyNight (Access Group): table bookings and card holds
  • Brevo: newsletter sign-up and email sending
  • Our email provider [e.g. Google Workspace / Microsoft 365], for enquiries
  • Our accountant and card-payment provider [names]

Other services we link to are run by companies that are independent data controllers. When you use them, their own privacy policies apply:

  • Deliveroo: delivery orders
  • Good Eats: click & collect orders
  • GiftPro: gift vouchers
  • Saint Paul’s Market: ordering and payment at our market stall go through the market’s own QR-code system
  • Instagram, TikTok and Facebook: if you follow or message us there

We may also share information if the law requires it, for example with environmental health officers or the police.

4. Our website and cookies

Our own website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, so we don’t show a cookie banner.

  • Hosting: our host [e.g. Netlify / Cloudflare / Vercel] keeps standard server logs, including IP address, browser type and pages requested, for a short time for security.
  • Fonts: our typefaces load from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google’s servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Booking page: the DesignMyNight booking form on our booking page is loaded from DesignMyNight and may set cookies needed to complete your booking. See DesignMyNight’s own cookie policy for details.

If we add analytics or marketing tools in future, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

6. Transfers outside the UK

Some of our providers, such as Google and some email services, may process data outside the UK. When they do, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or approved safeguards such as the International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • access a copy of your personal information
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • ask us to delete it
  • restrict or object to how we use it, including at any time for marketing
  • data portability: receive it in a reusable format
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent

To use any of these rights, email hello@tigerbitespig.co.uk. We will reply within one month. It’s free in most cases.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint , or call 0303 123 1113.

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date at the top.
